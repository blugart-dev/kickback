# Kickback — Full Audit (2026-09-12)

Scope: every file under `addons/kickback/`, `demo/`, `test/`, `docs/`, CI, git history.
Method: full read of the physics core (spring resolver, rig builder, rig sync, the
1,855-line controller, IK solvers), targeted reads of everything else, the GUT suite run
headless on Godot 4.7.2 + Jolt (176/176 pass, 1 leaked `Skeleton3D`), and grep-verified
claims. Line numbers refer to `main` at `583d294`.

---

## 1. Executive verdict

**The project has a solid *rig* and a scripted *brain*.** The parts that talk to the
engine — rig building, rest-centred anatomical joint frames, the `SkeletonModifier3D`
sync, the role-based profile, the two-bone IK math, the test harness — are good and
should be kept. The parts that are supposed to make it Euphoria — the muscle layer, the
balance model, and the 0.4.0 "self-preservation" behaviors — are not what the docs say
they are:

- The **muscle layer overwrites rigid-body velocities every tick**. It is a kinematic
  tracker with damping, not a muscle model. Hit impulses are erased within 2–3 ticks
  unless the script also lowers "strength". Every visible reaction is therefore a
  scripted strength schedule, not physics.
- The **directed stumble teleports the character root** (`_character_root.global_position
  += …`, controller:463). Feet have collision off in NORMAL/STAGGER and the pelvis is
  position-pinned, so the legs never bear load. "The legs step to keep it upright" is
  false; the pin keeps it upright.
- The **windmill is a canned circle** whose radius is scaled by a scripted decay value,
  not by balance error.
- The **balance model is static** (CoM offset / half stance width). No extrapolated CoM,
  no support polygon, no capture point. The tip-over decision is late by construction.
- **A limp ragdoll falls at 0.5 g.** `spring_resolver.gd:191` sets
  `gravity_scale = (1 - ratio) * 0.5`, so at strength 0 gravity is halved.
  `RagdollTuning.gravity_scale` (0.8, and 1.2 in `create_game_default`) is written once at
  build and overwritten on the first tick — a dead knob.

Honest parity: the roadmap's "~30 %" credits 0.4.0 as the first active survival layer. As
built it is a scripted displacement with real IK plumbing underneath. I would put real
parity at **~20–25 %**, and — more importantly — **the current muscle substrate caps what is
reachable.** You cannot build balance recovery, bracing, or grabbing on a layer that
overwrites velocities, because those behaviors are *defined* by bounded torques
interacting with contacts and gravity.

Recommendation (detail in §8): keep the rig, profile, IK math, sync, tests, resources;
**replace the muscle layer and decompose the controller**. This is a rewrite of roughly
2 of 7.5 k plugin lines, not a restart.

## 3. Architecture findings

### 3.1 The spring resolver is not a muscle model (`spring_resolver.gd`)

`_apply_angular_spring` (l. 274): `target_vel = axis * angle * 60 + ff_rot/delta`, then
`body.angular_velocity = lerp(current, target_vel, w(strength))`. Same for linear (l. 224).

Consequences:

1. **Mass and inertia are ignored.** A 15 kg pelvis and a 1 kg hand respond identically
   to the same strength value. "Strength" is a blend fraction, not a force.
2. **Impulses are erased.** A hit adds Δω; the next tick removes `strength` of it, then
   `strength` of the remainder. At Hips 0.65 a hit is 96 % gone in 3 ticks. That is why
   every reaction path must also drop strength, and why micro-reactions exist.
3. **It fights the constraint solver.** The joint solve rewrites the commanded velocity
   (their own measurement: 50–90 % of the chest's, a head's reversed). The
   `spring_chain_consistency` term (l. 232–252) is a clever patch that commands what the
   joint will enforce anyway — but it also **scales the child position pin to zero**, so
   the only thing holding the body up is the Hips pin.
4. **It fights contacts.** Velocity overwrite pushes bodies through/into contacts with
   unbounded effective force; the settle deadbands (l. 219, 289) exist to hide the
   resulting buzz. Feet collision is turned *off* in the standing states
   (`foot_ik_solver.gd:470`) for the same reason.
5. **Gravity is faked** (l. 191): off at full strength, half at zero. The body never has
   to hold itself up, so "strength" never had to mean anything physical. This is also why
   a dead ragdoll looks floaty.
6. **Frame-rate independence** is real for the lerp weight (`_fr_weight`), but the 60 Hz
   reference bakes a 1-tick-closure target into the design; the velocity clamps
   (`max_angular_velocity` 20 rad/s) then interact with tick rate. Not tested at 30/120.

The velocity approach is a legitimate way to get *an* active ragdoll on screen fast, and
GODOT_CONSTRAINTS.md records a good reason it was chosen (6DOF *springs* misbehave under
Jolt). But the same doc says **6DOF angular motors DO work under Jolt**. Motors with a
force limit are exactly a torque-bounded muscle. That is the path (§8).

### 3.2 Directed stumble is a root teleport (`active_ragdoll_controller.gd:451–489`)

- l. 463: `_character_root.global_position += _stumble_dir * _stumble_drift * delta`.
- Steps fire on distance travelled by that push (l. 470), not on balance.
- `_stumbling` **suspends the tip-over check** (l. 425) — the one place physics could
  say "no, you fell".
- On a `CharacterBody3D` root this bypasses collision (walks through walls); on a
  nav-driven NPC it fights the navigation agent.
- SELF_PRESERVATION.md is candid that emergent stepping "was invisible and unreliable"
  and was replaced by a scripted displacement. That is a diagnosis of the *muscle layer*
  (a foot reposition cannot read because the legs bear no load), not a reason to script
  the stumble.

### 3.3 Balance model (`_compute_balance_state`, l. 1490)

`ratio = |CoM_xz − mean(feet)_xz| / max(furthest foot distance, 0.1)`. Static, isotropic,
and the "radius" is half the stance width in every direction (so a forward lean is
measured against sideways foot spread). `_com_velocity` is computed (l. 1758) but only
used for a tensing multiplier. There is no XCoM (`CoM + v/√(g/l)`), no support polygon
from foot geometry, no "which foot is loaded" from contact. Every self-preservation
behavior on the roadmap needs those three numbers.

### 3.4 Stagger sway is a sine wave (`_apply_stagger_sway`, l. 1817)

Two sines at irrational ratios plus a twist torque, applied as forces to the hips and
torso. It is "juice", and because the resolver overwrites velocity each tick, the force's
actual effect depends on the strength ratio in a way nobody can tune by reasoning.

### 3.5 The controller is a god object with no arbitration

1,855 lines: state machine, hit handling, fatigue/pain/injury, balance, active
resistance, directional bracing, sway, stumble, windmill, fall brace, guided persistent,
recovery teleport, budget. **Twenty `set_bone_strength` call sites** across
floor-enforcement, resistance, pulses, stumble brace, fall brace, guide, recovery ramp,
foot-IK leg strength. Effective per-bone strength is whatever ran last this tick. Each new
feature PR added another writer (git: 46 touches on this file). There is no notion of
"behavior A wants stiffness s with priority p"; you cannot add wall bracing or grabbing to
this without adding a 21st writer.

### 3.6 Initialization by await-counting

`PhysicsRigBuilder._ready` awaits 2 process frames, `SpringResolver._ready` 3,
`KickbackCharacter._ready` 5 (`kickback_character.gd:94`), and INTEGRATION.md documents
the 5-frame wait as a contract. The controller lazily builds IK solvers once the resolver
has bones. This works because the numbers happen to be ordered; it is fragile to scene
structure, `process_mode`, and pausing. `_build_adjacency` runs once in `_ready` and is
not re-run by `configure()`.

### 3.7 IK ordering and drift (from the IK sub-audit)

- Overrides written by foot/arm IK are consumed **next tick** (setup tool creates
  SpringResolver before the controller; no `process_physics_priority`).
  `foot_ik_solver.gd:65–73` and FOOT_IK.md claim same-frame.
- **Root-motion stripping is bypassed** whenever the pelvis drop is active: the override
  path skips the XZ zeroing (`spring_resolver.gd:207–213`).
- `TwoBoneIK.solve` returns `{}` when the target is outside `[min, max+0.1]`
  (`two_bone_ik.gd:314`); callers then write **no override**, so the limb snaps to the
  animation pose. The windmill centre sits ~0.19 m from the shoulder with radius up to
  0.24 m, so the arm pops every cycle.
- Degeneracy test is post-normalisation (`two_bone_ik.gd:327–331`): a near-straight
  idle knee can flip its bend plane frame to frame.
- Fallback pole vector ignores `character_forward_sign` (`foot_ik_solver.gd:420`,
  `arm_ik_solver.gd:232`) — knees bend backward on +Z-facing models when the animation
  leg is straight.
- Segment lengths are measured on the **left** limb only and reused for the right.

## 5. API surface

`RagdollTuning`: **139 exported properties in 15 groups**, "Hit Reactions" alone has 50
covering stagger, sway, pain, injury, micro-reactions, movement and threat. It is a log of
feature PRs, not a tuning model. Interactions a user cannot see from the inspector:

- `knockdown_enabled = false` no-ops four other knobs.
- Everything in Collision / Advanced: Physics / `joint_limit_scale` is build-time only.
- `recovery_rate` is not yours (see defect 5).
- Sway falloffs are applied by position in `torso_rigs`, not by name as documented.

`KickbackCharacter`: config duplicated 4× (`skeleton_path` on four nodes,
`character_root_path` on two, resolved with different null-safety). Vestigial `Mode`
enum with two values. `Mode.NONE` is not a mode.

The runtime API is incomplete: the 45-line node assembly every runtime spawner needs
lives in `demo/demo_helpers.gd`, not the plugin.

## 8. Recommendation

### Keep as-is (with the §4 fixes)
`physics_rig_builder.gd` (the joint-frame work is the best code in the repo),
`physics_rig_sync.gd`, `ragdoll_profile.gd` + roles, `joint_definition.gd`,
`bone_definition.gd`, `impact_profile.gd`, `two_bone_ik.gd`, the foot-IK raycast /
pelvis-drop core, `kickback_manager.gd`, `kickback_raycast.gd`, `test/helpers/rig_harness.gd`
and the real half of the suite.

> **Spike outcome (same day, [MUSCLE_SPIKE.md](MUSCLE_SPIKE.md)):** velocity-mode
> motors confirmed as the substrate; position-mode springs and script PD rejected on
> data; the resolver's command formulation is kept and executed through the motors
> ("evolve, not replace"). The plan below stands with that amendment.

### Replace: the muscle layer
Drive each jointed body through its `Generic6DOFJoint3D` **angular motor** (target
angular velocity in the joint frame, `PARAM_ANGULAR_MOTOR_FORCE_LIMIT` = muscle strength
in N·m). GODOT_CONSTRAINTS.md already records motors as working under Jolt; the joint
frames are already rest-centred and anatomical, so per-axis targets are well defined. The
velocity-target computation (error × gain + feed-forward) survives almost verbatim; it
just stops being *written to the body* and becomes a *motor target* the solver enforces
with bounded torque. Then:

- Gravity stays at 1.0 always. Strength means torque. A hit that exceeds the muscle's
  torque limit *is* a reaction — no strength schedule required for the first-order effect.
- Contacts and joints are solved together with the muscles; the deadbands, chain-
  consistency patch and self-collision-off default become unnecessary (keep
  self-collision off anyway for cost).
- The root (Hips) has no parent joint: give it an "upright" PD torque via
  `apply_torque` with a cap plus a *weak* position pin as a safety net, not the 0.85
  kinematic anchor it is now.
- Fallback if motor axis semantics fight you: script PD torque via `apply_torque`,
  gains normalised by `PhysicsDirectBodyState3D.inverse_inertia_tensor`, clamped. Also
  legitimate; slightly less stable at high stiffness.

**Spike first (2–3 days):** one arm chain on the ybot, motor-driven, A/B against the
resolver on tracking error, hit response, and 30/120 Hz. Decide on data.

### Replace: the balance model
`BalanceState` = CoM, CoM velocity, XCoM (`CoM + v / √(g / leg_length)`), support polygon
from foot *contact* geometry (feet collide again), loaded foot from contact impulses, and
the signed distance of XCoM to the polygon edge. One struct, computed once per tick,
consumed by everything.

### Decompose the controller into behaviors with arbitration
```
Behavior.tick(balance, delta) -> {joint targets (optional), stiffness scale per bone, priority}
Arbiter: highest priority per bone wins; ties blend
```
Behaviors: `Upright` (root PD), `Step` (XCoM outside polygon → swing-leg IK target →
leg joint targets, body moves because the loaded leg pushes), `ArmBalance` (arm target
opposes XCoM error — replaces the phase circle), `FallReach` (keep; it is already the
right shape), `Flinch`, `GuidedDeath`, `GetUp` (canned blend, stays canned). The state
machine becomes ~200 lines that enable/disable behaviors. Delete the root teleport, the
sine sway, and the 20 direct strength writes.

### Tuning surface
Cut `RagdollTuning` to the knobs that map to a physical or behavioral quantity (muscle
torque per bone group, upright gain, step trigger margin, step duration, …). Move
build-time knobs to the profile. Target ≤ 40 exports.

### Order of work
1. **Honesty pass** (days): fix ROADMAP/SELF_PRESERVATION/README claims, delete dead
   knobs, §4 items 1–5, 12–15, 19–22. Tag 0.4.1.
2. **Muscle spike** (≤ 1 week), decision.
3. **Muscle layer + gravity on + feet load-bearing + upright root** behind a
   `muscle_mode` switch, ship when tracking error ≤ current on the ybot. 0.5.0.
4. **BalanceState + Step + ArmBalance** as behaviors; remove the scripted stumble. 0.6.0.
5. **Arbiter + controller slimming + tuning cut + API freeze**. 0.7.0.
6. Wall bracing / grabbing / crawling then become behaviors on a substrate that can
   support them.

Estimated size: ~2,000–2,500 lines replaced, the rest kept. The rig and the tests make
this tractable; the reason to do it now is that every milestone after 0.4.0 on the current
roadmap would be another scripted layer on a substrate that cannot carry it.
